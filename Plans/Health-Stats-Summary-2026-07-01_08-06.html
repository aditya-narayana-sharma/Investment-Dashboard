Read nutrition trends with care

Dietary energy, carbs, sugar and water show very large "vs first week" jumps mainly because logging ramped up over the month — early-July days have sparse or zero nutrition entries. These are logged entries, not verified total intake, so treat the direction as a completeness signal rather than a real change in what was consumed.
